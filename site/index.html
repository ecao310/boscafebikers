Your first ride

Never ridden with a group before? This is the one to start with.

  • No-drop, always. Nobody gets left behind.
  • All levels welcome. Most rides are 3-5 flat-ish miles at a conversational pace.
  • BlueBikes are perfect. Any bike that rolls is the right bike. Some Bluebikes coupons available.
  • Bring a lock, a water bottle, and enough cash or card for a coffee.
  • Helmets encouraged, lights required if we're out near sunset.

About us

Boston Café Bikers is a casual cycling group built around the two best things about this city: the riding and the coffee. We meet up somewhere reachable by T or bike, roll out together, and finish at a café.

There's no membership, no dues, and no drop. Just show up, say hi, and order something warm at the end.

Ride announcements go out on Partiful. Photos and route teasers go on Instagram.

Instagram — @bostoncafebikers Partiful — all our events

Contact us

Starting a café bike club in your own city? Want to team up or partner with ours? Say hi.

The fastest ways to reach us are email, a DM on Instagram, or a message in the WhatsApp group. Or roll out with us on a ride and talk over coffee at the end.
